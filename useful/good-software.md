# Good software

A running list of software I've come across that's worth remembering: interesting,
well-made, or quietly excellent. Curiosity welcome.

## PostHog

Open-source, all-in-one product analytics. One platform instead of stitching five
tools together: product analytics (events, funnels, retention), session replay,
feature flags, A/B experiments, surveys, and a built-in data warehouse.

Why it's on the list:
- **Open source + self-hostable**, with a genuinely generous free tier.
- **Developer-first**: you instrument with a small SDK and get most of the toolkit
  at once, rather than buying Amplitude + LaunchDarkly + Hotjar + Optimizely separately.
- **Strong engineering culture** — their public handbook and engineering blog are a
  good read in their own right, and the product iterates fast.

- Link: https://posthog.com
